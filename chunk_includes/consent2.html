Information Sheet for Participation in a Research Study

Principal Investigator: Dr. Abby Walker

IRB# and Title of Study: #26-496 Language and Research

WHAT SHOULD I KNOW?

The purpose of this study is to understand how people process language, language users, and language research. You will either be played spoken or see written samples of language and be asked questions about the language or the speaker/author. You will also be asked to complete a questionnaire about language and research, and some demographic information. The experiment will take no more than 30 minutes.

We do not anticipate any risks from completing this study.

You can choose whether to be in this study or not. If you volunteer to be in this study, you may withdraw at any time without consequences of any kind. You may also refuse to answer any questions you don’t want to answer and remain in the study.

You will be given $15 an hour for participating in this study, allotted in ten minute increments.

Textual/numerical data collected from this experiment may be made available to other researchers to fulfill scholarly obligations to open science, but all data will be preserved or distributed in a completely anonymous format with no personal identification marks.

CONFIDENTIALITY

We will do our best to protect the confidentiality of the information we gather from you, but we cannot guarantee 100% confidentiality.

Your responses will not be connected with your name by our research team. Please do not include your name or other identifying information in your responses that can identify you.

WHO CAN I TALK TO?

If you have any questions or concerns about the research, please feel free to contact Dr. Abby Walker (ajwalker@vt.edu). You are not waiving any legal claims, rights or remedies because of your participation in this research study. If you have questions regarding your rights as a research participant, contact the Virginia Tech HRPP Office at 540-231-3732 (irb@vt.edu).
